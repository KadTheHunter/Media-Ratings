Ramblings & Musings

The Inevitable Decline of Long-Running Shows

"Example #76193 of Hollywood being creatively bankrupt"

Any show with a linear storyline that runs longer than 3 seasons will inevitably have a disappointing ending, and anything stretching past 5-6 seasons is guaranteed a terrible ending. I have yet to find any show other than a sitcom or anthology that can survive past 3 seasons without quickly declining into slop.

Show Selection Process

"but I can't watch Ozark after The Blacklist, I need a palate cleanser!"

In my usual over-thinking it style, I try to alternate my media consooooming by an even balance of Genre, Length and Setting/Time Period. I can't follow a long Modern-Day Crime Thriller with another long Modern-Day Crime Thriller, it has to be a Western, or maybe a short Victorian Drama.
